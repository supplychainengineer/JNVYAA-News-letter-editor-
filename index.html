    Section types are detected from the rows (dates → events, numbers → stats, photos → spotlight). Force one with {stats}, {events}, {quote}, {people}, {chart}, {table}, {gallery} or {cta} after a heading.

    CSV with columns like type, heading, body, image, date, value, label works too, as does a plain two-column spreadsheet (becomes a chart).

    Photos you upload but don't mention are placed automatically: cover first, then stories without a picture, the rest in a gallery.

    Reference a photo by its file name, e.g. ![Caption](reunion.jpg). Click Insert to add it at the cursor.
