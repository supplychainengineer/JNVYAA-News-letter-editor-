## Silver Jubilee Reunion brings 600 alumni home
### Cover story
![The main quad, dressed up for the jubilee weekend](campus.svg)
On a bright October weekend, the old campus filled with laughter that hadn't echoed through its corridors in years. Batches from 1996 to 2021 travelled from 14 countries to walk the same paths, eat in the same dining hall and, for one evening, answer roll call one more time.

The programme opened with a tree-planting by the founding batch, followed by an exhibition of archival photographs curated by current students. By evening, the amphitheatre was packed for a cultural night that ran well past midnight.

"It felt like the campus had been waiting for us," said one attendee from the 2003 batch. Planning for the next reunion has already begun — and the organising committee is looking for volunteers.

## Scholarship fund crosses ₹1 crore
Thanks to 340 donors, the endowment now funds 24 full scholarships every year. The first recipients under the expanded programme start in June.

## Mentorship circles are back
Forty alumni have signed up to mentor final-year students through 2027. Circles meet monthly online and cover careers, entrance exams and life after school.
